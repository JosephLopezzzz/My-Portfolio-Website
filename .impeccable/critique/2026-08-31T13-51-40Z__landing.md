#### Overall Impression
The portfolio looks stunning and premium. The visual polish is there, but the functional layer (like navigation active states and form validation) could use a bit more robustness to match the visual quality. The single biggest opportunity is making the navigation feel more alive and reactive to the user's scroll position.

#### What's Working
- **The Glass Aesthetic**: The cards and form inputs blend beautifully into the background, creating a deep, layered effect.
- **Section Spacing**: The 6rem padding lets each section breathe, making the content feel deliberate and important.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Invisible Scroll Status**: Users scrolling down the long page have no visual indication in the sticky navbar of which section they are currently viewing.
  - **Why it matters**: It breaks heuristic #1 (Visibility of System Status) and makes the navigation feel disconnected from the content.
  - **Fix**: Implement an intersection observer to highlight the active navigation link based on scroll position.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P2] Missing "Back to Top"**: On a long single-page portfolio, getting back to the hero/navigation takes a lot of manual scrolling on mobile.
  - **Why it matters**: Reduces user control and freedom for mobile users (`Casey`).
  - **Fix**: Add a floating, glass-styled "arrow-up" button that appears after scrolling down.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: 
- Long single-page scroll with no quick way back to the top.
- The contact form is at the very bottom; if they get interrupted mid-scroll, they might miss the call to action entirely.

**Riley (Deliberate Stress Tester)**:
- Submitting the contact form with empty or invalid data only triggers native browser tooltips, which look out of place against the premium glass UI. No custom validation styling.

#### Minor Observations
- The "All Technologies" block in the Skills section shows every skill at once, which slightly increases cognitive load compared to the tabbed view above it.

#### Questions to Consider
- Does the "All Technologies" section add enough value to justify the visual density, or should we rely solely on the categorized tabs?
- Should the contact form have real-time, custom-styled validation to match the premium feel?
