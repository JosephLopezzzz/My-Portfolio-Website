# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Vite, React, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, Shadcn UI

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients reviewing my portfolio for full-time employment or freelance work.

## Product Purpose
To showcase my technical skills, projects, and professional experience to secure employment or freelance contracts. Success means engaging the visitor enough to reach out.

## Positioning
My core focus is on technical excellence and complex problem-solving.

## Operating Context
Visitors will likely view this portfolio on desktop or mobile browsers, often scanning quickly to assess technical competence, past projects, and contact information.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The portfolio must clearly present resume content and project links.

## Brand Commitments
I am open to full visual redesigns. There are no mandatory brand colors or logos.

## Evidence on Hand
My existing resume content, certifications, and project links.
